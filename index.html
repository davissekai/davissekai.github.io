01 / about

Hello, I'm Davis.

I work across AI, carbon, and ecosystem services, mostly in Ghana.

I'm an MPhil student in Climate Change and Sustainable Development at the University of Ghana's Centre for Climate Change and Sustainability Studies. My research asks how carbon markets and MRV can make forests and other ecosystems worth more standing than cleared, and who gets to share in that value.

My background is Industrial Engineering at KNUST. I spent my national service with the Africa Climate Collaborative, working alongside climate entrepreneurs and innovation hubs from Accra to Tamale.

I also make AI-enabled products: satellite and AI experiments, learning games, and small utilities for people who work with AI agents. Each one starts with a question I can't let go of.
